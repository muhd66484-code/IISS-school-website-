Welcome to Intisharul Islam Secondary School

Intisharul Islam Secondary School (IISS) is a secondary school located in Yelwan Tudu, Bauchi, Bauchi State, Nigeria.

Our goal is to provide students with quality education, sound character development, discipline and the knowledge and skills needed to contribute positively to society.
