ABOUT IISS

Welcome to Intisharul Islam Secondary School

Intisharul Islam Secondary School (IISS) is a secondary school located in Yelwan Tudu, Bauchi, Bauchi State, Nigeria.

Our goal is to provide students with a strong educational foundation while encouraging discipline, good character, creativity, responsibility and lifelong learning.
